Code of Practice for Online Safety — Social Media Services

In force Infocomm Media Development Authority Content takedown Transparency reporting Age assurance

Commenced 18 Jul 2023

Obligations

Designated social media services must maintain systems and processes to mitigate exposure to harmful content, including minor-specific safeguards and reporting tools.
general · policy, product, ops
Evidence: Systems documentation, tooling screenshots, process descriptions
Submit an annual online safety compliance report to IMDA describing measures taken and their effectiveness.
general · policy, data, legal
Clock: Annual
Evidence: Report source metrics with documented lineage back to production systems

Penalty exposure

Up to SGD 1,000,000

Fixed sum under the Broadcasting Act for non-compliance with directions

Access-blocking orders

Facebook and Instagram are among the designated social media services subject to the Code.

The annual report is the recurring transparency obligation most likely to expose a data-lineage gap — the report asks questions internal metrics were not shaped to answer. Re-verified 2026-10-13: no change found. IMDA's designated services list and the annual Online Safety Assessment Report cycle are unchanged.

Online Safety (Relief and Accountability) Act 2025

Partially in force Online Safety Commission Content takedown

Passed 05 Nov 2025 · Commenced 29 Jun 2026

Obligations

Platforms may receive directions from the Commission requiring removal, restriction of access, or disabling of accounts in respect of specified online harms.
Part 3 · legal, ops, policy
Clock: Direction-specific; set by the Commission on issue
Evidence: Direction register, action taken, timestamps, and reinstatement decisions
Statutory torts define duties for Communicators, Administrators and Platforms, creating a civil route to relief independent of regulator action.
Part 2 · legal, policy
Evidence: Records sufficient to respond to a civil claim or identity-disclosure order

Penalty exposure

Non-compliance with a direction is an offence; civil liability under the statutory torts

Access-disabling and account-restriction directions

Enforcement signals

29 Jun 2026 — OSC begins first phase covering harassment, doxxing, stalking, intimate image abuse, image-based child abuse [source]

Applies to social media services accessible in Singapore. No platform-specific designation published at commencement.

Newest regulator in the region. First operating year means process and escalation thresholds are still forming — highest-variance relationship in APAC. Re-verified 2026-10-13: no change found; OSC remains in its first operating year.

Personal Data Protection Act 2012

In force Personal Data Protection Commission Data protection

Commenced 02 Jul 2014

Obligations

Notify the Commission of a notifiable data breach as soon as practicable, and in any case within 3 calendar days of assessing it to be notifiable.
Part 6A · legal, security, data
Clock: 3 calendar days to the Commission
Evidence: Assessment record establishing when the breach was determined notifiable

Penalty exposure

Up to 10% of annual turnover in Singapore, or SGD 1,000,000, whichever is higher

Turnover percentage with a floor

Directions to stop collection or destroy data

Applies to organisations processing personal data in Singapore.

The 3-day clock runs from the assessment decision, not from discovery — which makes the assessment timestamp the single most contested fact in any breach filing. Re-verified 2026-10-13: no change found.
