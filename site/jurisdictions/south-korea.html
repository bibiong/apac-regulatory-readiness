Personal Information Protection Act — 2026 Amendments

개인정보 보호법

In force Personal Information Protection Commission Data protection

Passed 10 Mar 2026 · Commenced 11 Sep 2026

Obligations

Notify the Commission without delay on becoming aware of the possibility of a breach — a lower and earlier trigger than confirmed-breach standards elsewhere.
general · legal, security, ops
Clock: Without delay from awareness of possibility
Evidence: Awareness timeline. The trigger is suspicion, so the record must show what was known and when, not merely what was later confirmed.
Personal supervisory liability attaches to the head of the institution for systemic compliance failures.
general · legal, policy
Evidence: Governance records demonstrating executive oversight
Stricter conditions on overseas transfers of personal data.
general · legal, engineering, data
Evidence: Transfer impact assessments and routing documentation

Penalty exposure

Up to 10% of total annual turnover

Baseline remains 3% of relevant revenue. A punitive track permits up to 10% of total turnover for systemic failures — repeat serious violations within three years, incidents affecting more than 10 million individuals, or blatant failure to follow previous regulatory orders.

Personal liability in scope.

Corrective orders

Applies to controllers processing Korean personal data, including foreign platforms.

Status moved from passed to in_force on re-verification 2026-09-15: secondary sources consistently give 11 September 2026 as the commencement date for most provisions and no postponement has been reported. Commencement has not yet been confirmed against a PIPC primary notice, so confidence stays medium. Was the highest-priority readiness item in the tracker on time-to-obligation. Turnover-based fines plus personal liability convert a legal risk into a CFO- and CEO-visible risk, which changes the internal escalation path, not just the exposure.
