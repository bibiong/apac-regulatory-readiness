Draft amendments to Decree 147 on minors' use of internet services

Consultation Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism Age assurance

Obligations

As revised in September 2026, under-16s are not barred from social media, but a parent or guardian must register and supervise the account; under-13s would be barred from posting, commenting or sharing.
general · product, engineering, policy
As drafted, game operators would verify user age, register under-16 accounts through a parent or guardian, and cap under-16 play at 60 minutes per day.
general · product, engineering
Would apply to social media services serving Vietnamese users.

Consultation stage — a shaping opportunity rather than a compliance obligation, and the workstream most often under-resourced because it carries a soft deadline. Re-verified 2026-10-13: the draft has materially changed direction since this entry was written. The August version recorded a posting ban for under-16s; September reporting has the ministry stepping back from that to parental registration and supervision, with the posting ban falling to under-13s. Still consultation stage. This is the clearest example in the tracker of why a draft entry needs a short re-verification cycle — the obligation recorded in August would now be wrong in both scope and age threshold.
