Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

Partially in force Data Protection Board of India Data protection Age assurance

Passed 11 Aug 2023

Obligations

Verifiable parental consent required before processing the personal data of a child under 18; targeted advertising to children restricted.
s.9 · legal, product, policy
Evidence: Consent artefacts and verification method documentation
Notify the Board and each affected Data Principal of a personal data breach.
s.8(6) · legal, security, comms
Clock: Prescribed by rules
Evidence: Breach register and notification records

Penalty exposure

Up to INR 250 crore per instance

Fixed sum per contravention category

Applies to significant data fiduciaries operating in India.

Phased implementation. Under-18 consent threshold is materially stricter than the under-16 line used in AU and ID — a design constraint that does not generalise. Re-verified 2026-10-13: no change found.

Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026

In force Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology Content takedown AI & synthetic media

Passed 10 Feb 2026 · Commenced 20 Feb 2026

Obligations

Remove or disable access to court-flagged deepfake content within 3 hours of notification.
general · ops, legal, engineering
Clock: 3 hours
Evidence: Receipt timestamp, action timestamp, and the decision basis
Remove non-consensual sexual imagery within a 2-hour window.
general · ops, legal
Clock: 2 hours
Evidence: Receipt-to-action timestamps; escalation path taken
Synthetically generated information must carry a prominent label and permanent provenance metadata that cannot be stripped.
general · product, engineering, policy
Evidence: Labelling implementation spec; metadata persistence testing
Significant Social Media Intermediaries must require users to declare whether content is synthetically generated before publishing, and verify that declaration through an automated process rather than accepting it at face value.
general · product, engineering, policy
Evidence: Declaration flow design; automated verification accuracy metrics
Issue periodic user advisories setting out the consequences of non-compliance, at least once every three months.
general · policy, comms, product
Clock: Every 3 months
Evidence: Advisory text, distribution surface and send dates per quarter

Penalty exposure

Missing a statutory removal window is treated as a due-diligence failure capable of stripping safe harbour under s.79 of the IT Act 2000.

Applies to significant social media intermediaries, a threshold Meta's services exceed.

The compounding case matters: a synthetic asset built from a real person's likeness triggers content-labelling duties and data-protection consent duties at once — one artefact, two regimes, two clocks. A 3-hour clock cannot accommodate a cross-functional review, so the decision must be pre-made: named on-call SME per surface and a pre-approved decision tree. Re-verified 2026-10-13: added the quarterly user-advisory duty, which the initial entry missed. The labelling standard is qualitative (prominent, noticeable, perceivable; audio disclosure prefixed) rather than the proposed 10%-surface-area watermark.
