Information Distribution Platform Act

情報流通プラットフォーム対処法

In force Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications Content takedown Transparency reporting Licensing

Obligations

Designated large-scale platform operators must expedite responses to rights-infringement notices and publish transparency disclosures.
Art. 20(1) · legal, ops, policy, data
Clock: Expedited response to rights-infringement notices
Evidence: Enforcement disclosure metrics with documented lineage. Completeness is the supervised standard, not merely publication.

Penalty exposure

Corrective recommendations; designation-linked supervision

Enforcement signals

24 Jul 2026 — MIC issued corrective recommendations to five designated operators — X Corp., Google, Meta Platforms, Shonan Seibu Home (bakusai.com) and Dwango (Niconico) — finding FY2025 enforcement disclosures incomplete against a ministerial ordinance. MIC may issue formal corrective orders if the recommendations are not met without legitimate grounds. [source]

Meta is among the designated large-scale operators and publishes an annual IDPA transparency report covering Facebook, Instagram and Threads.

Reporting-completeness findings are usually a data-lineage problem rather than a willingness problem — the report asks a question internal metrics were never shaped to answer.
