# Pre-submission QA checklist

Complete before approval. Every item is either **pass**, or **fail with a named owner and
a fix time**. There is no "N/A" without a written reason — the whole point of a checklist
is that it resists the pressure to skip under deadline.

## 1. Answering the question actually asked

- [ ] Every question in the inquiry maps to a numbered section in the response.
- [ ] No question is answered by implication, or answered adjacent to what was asked.
- [ ] Where the answer is "we do not know" or "we do not yet comply", it says so plainly.
      A regulator who later discovers a hedge treats every other answer differently.
- [ ] Scope of the answer (products, date range, geography) is stated explicitly and matches
      the scope the regulator specified — not the scope that was convenient to produce.

## 2. Cross-jurisdictional consistency

- [ ] Prior filings on this topic, in **every** jurisdiction, retrieved and read.
- [ ] Any figure that differs from a previously filed figure for the same population is
      either reconciled or explained in-line.
- [ ] Definitions are consistent with prior use, or the change is flagged and dated.
      A silently redefined metric is the single most common source of apparent contradiction.
- [ ] Positional statements ("we consider X to be Y") checked against policy positions held
      in other markets.

## 3. Numbers and lineage

- [ ] Every figure traces to a named query or system of record, recorded in the working file.
- [ ] Date ranges stated for every metric, and consistent across the document.
- [ ] Rounding and estimation disclosed where used.
- [ ] Someone other than the author has re-derived at least one figure independently.

## 4. Commitments

- [ ] Every forward-looking statement identified and listed.
- [ ] Each has a named owner and a date.
- [ ] Each is resourced, or explicitly flagged as unresourced to the approver **before** filing.
- [ ] No commitment is made in a tense or phrasing that binds more than intended
      ("we will" vs "we currently intend to").

## 5. Legal and disclosure posture

- [ ] Privilege reviewed; nothing privileged included inadvertently.
- [ ] Document drafted on the assumption it may be published or tabled in parliament.
- [ ] Personal data minimised to what the inquiry requires.
- [ ] Confidentiality claims marked in the form the regulator accepts — not merely asserted.

## 6. Filing mechanics

- [ ] Filed through the channel the regulator specified, in the format specified.
- [ ] Deadline confirmed in the regulator's timezone, not the drafter's.
- [ ] Receipt or acknowledgement captured and attached to the inquiry record.
- [ ] Inquiry record updated: status, filed timestamp, commitments logged.
