# Disclaimer

**This is not legal advice.** It is a personal portfolio project built to demonstrate
program-management method applied to regulatory work.

**It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any employer, past or present.**
Nothing here describes any organisation's internal processes. Where a company is named —
Meta, for instance — the statement is sourced to public reporting and cited, and appears
because a tracker of platform regulation that refuses to name the platforms it affects
would be useless.

**Fictional material is labelled as such.** The worked examples in `workflow/examples/` and
`readiness/worked-example/` are invented. "Northwind Social" does not exist. The regimes,
regulators, deadlines and enforcement signals those examples sit inside are real and cited;
the maturity scores, owners, internal estimates and inquiry correspondence are not.

**The regulatory data will go out of date.** Every instrument carries `last_verified` and a
`confidence` rating, and the validator warns when an entry passes its freshness window.
Those fields exist because a tracker that hides its own staleness is worse than no tracker.
Re-verify against the cited primary sources before relying on anything here.

**The author is not a lawyer.** The obligations recorded in this tracker are a program
manager's working summary intended to drive triage, scoping and sequencing — not a
statement of legal effect. Anything that matters should go to qualified counsel in-market.

**Constructed statutory text.** The excerpts in `toolkit/eval/gold/` are written in the
drafting style of the named instruments. They are not quotations. See the gold set README
for why, and for the limitation that creates.
